Plant diseases are a major threat to food security all over the world. In addition, it is a yield and quality constraint for growers of broadacre crops.
Plant pathogens can be viral, fungal or bacterial. They can damage plant parts above or below the ground. When agriculture tries to support the life of the growing population, plant disease reduces the production and quality of not only food and fiber but also biofuel crops at the same time.

This app is an image recognition system, which is able to detect and classify some diseases of such plants like:
• Apple,
• Blueberry,
• Cherry,
• Corn,
• Grape,
• Orange,
• Peach,
• Pepper, bell,
• Potato,
• Raspberry,
• Soybean,
• Squash,
• Strawberry,
• Tomato.
